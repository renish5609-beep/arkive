# Classroom pilot protocol

This protocol describes a short, optional activity. It has not been run. Run it only with a real class and an instructor who agrees to it.

## Format

- **Duration:** 15 to 30 minutes
- **Audience:** history or humanities students
- **Accounts:** none required. Students use the public site.
- **Data:** do not collect sensitive student data. Collect only the three answers below, without names.

## Activities

1. Inspect a source.
2. Inspect a reconstructed record.
3. Compare the evidence with the interpretation.
4. Inspect an unresolved location.
5. Answer three feedback questions.

## Feedback questions

1. Could you tell where a claim came from?
2. Did Arkive make uncertainty clear?
3. What was confusing or useful?

## After the pilot

Record the pilot in `data/project-impact.json` only if it actually happened, with a note beginning `classroom:` that describes the activity. Do not record the number of students unless the instructor reports it, and do not record student identities.
