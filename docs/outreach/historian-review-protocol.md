# Historian review protocol

Target time: 15 to 20 minutes. The reviewer may take the review asynchronously.

## Please focus on

1. **Framing of Quakertown.** Does the project describe the community, the displacement, and the 1920s context accurately, and without overstatement?
2. **Responsible use of oral history.** Are interviews described with their context, their speakers, and their limits?
3. **Provenance clarity.** Can you follow a claim from a record to its source, and tell what each source actually supports?
4. **Uncertainty representation.** Are unresolved locations, approximate locations, and open questions shown clearly?
5. **Entity resolution methodology.** Are candidate matches presented as proposals, with their conflicts, and never as settled identities?
6. **Georeference caution.** Does the map avoid implying more precision than the evidence supports?
7. **Omissions and misleading presentation.** What is missing, or what would a careful reader misunderstand?

## Notes for the reviewer

- The dataset is early. Most sources are short descriptions, and transcripts have not yet been read in full.
- Automated match scores are heuristics for prioritizing research. They are not historical findings.
- Your criticism is the most useful input. Please be specific about the page or record.
