# Review request

Thank you for reviewing Arkive. The review guide at [link] walks through the project in about five minutes. Your answers to the five questions below are the most useful thing you can send. Short answers are fine.

## Questions

1. **Is the historical framing accurate?**
   Consider the project description, the Quakertown context, and the person and place records you looked at. Note anything that overstates, understates, or misstates what the sources support.

2. **Is provenance clear?**
   Could you follow a claim from a record to its source? Did the source pages tell you what the source is and what it covers?

3. **Is uncertainty communicated responsibly?**
   Did the unresolved places, the approximate locations, and the open research questions make sense? Did anything look more certain than it should?

4. **What would make the tool useful to you?**
   Describe the task you would use it for, and what is missing for that task.

5. **What is missing or misleading?**
   Anything wrong, absent, or confusing. Please be specific about the page or record.

## How to send your answers

Reply to the email, or submit them through the [feedback page](/feedback). You can choose whether your name and affiliation appear publicly. Unless you give permission, responses are recorded without your name.

Thank you again. Reviews that disagree with the project are as useful as reviews that agree with it.
