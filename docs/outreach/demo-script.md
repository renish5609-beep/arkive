# Demo script (2 minutes)

Use this script for a live or recorded walkthrough. Everything it shows exists in the repository today. Do not describe partnerships, users, or classroom use that have not happened.

**Setup:** open the project at the homepage, then keep the [demo page](/demo) in a second tab.

## 0:00. What Arkive is (15 seconds)

"Arkive is an open-source platform that rebuilds communities from fragmented archival records. Every connection stays tied to its source, and uncertainty is shown rather than hidden."

## 0:15. Follow a source (25 seconds)

Open `/sources/source_005`, the Denton County Historical Commission narrative.

"Each source has its own page. This one lists the mentions extracted from it, and shows which ones are linked to a record and which are still unresolved."

## 0:40. See a reconstructed person (20 seconds)

Open `/records/person_003`, Frederick Douglass Moore.

"Each record shows its verification state, its sources, and its relationships. It also shows any open research question about this person."

## 1:00. Inspect provenance and location (30 seconds)

Open `/records/place_005`, the house at 1129 East Hickory Street.

"This is the one exact coordinate in the dataset. The record shows how the location was derived, who reviewed it, and what it does not establish. Compare it with `/records/place_001`, which has no marker because its location is not yet supported by the evidence."

## 1:30. Show the uncertainty (15 seconds)

Open `/review`.

"Arkive keeps its open questions visible. Pending identity matches stay pending until a person reviews them."

## 1:45. Download the data (15 seconds)

Open `/api/export/quakertown?format=json`.

"The full dataset downloads with source IDs and verification metadata intact, so anyone can check where a claim came from."

## Close

"Arkive is an early reconstruction, and most of the sources are still short descriptions. The point is to show the evidence and its limits, so that a historian can check each claim."
