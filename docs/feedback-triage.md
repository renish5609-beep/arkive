# Feedback triage

Every piece of incoming feedback is classified before anyone acts on it. The class decides how urgently it is handled.

## Classes

- **A. Historical factual correction.** A claim, date, name, or relationship is wrong.
- **B. Provenance or source issue.** A source is missing, misdescribed, unavailable, or does not support a claim.
- **C. Georeference issue.** A location is misplaced, or its derivation is questioned.
- **D. Usability issue.** Something is hard to find, read, or use.
- **E. Feature request.** A capability that does not yet exist.
- **F. General praise or comment.**

## Response rules

- **A to C:** prioritize verification. Check the cited source before changing any record. Do not change a historical claim on a reviewer's word alone.
- **D:** fix it if it blocks understanding or use.
- **E:** do not build it automatically. Assess it against the project's mission and record the assessment.
- **F:** record it only if it is useful. Do not turn praise into a metric.

Do not let one reviewer trigger major architecture work without a concrete need.

## Recording

For each item, note the class, the review ID (if any), the decision, and the commit or issue that resulted. Keep private reviewer details out of public issues.
